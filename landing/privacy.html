Privacy Policy

Compass · compass.nautilus.social · Last updated: 2026-09-06

1. What we collect

  • Account data — the email address you register, and your passphrase stored only as a one-way hash (never in plaintext).
  • API tokens — stored only as SHA-256 hashes; the raw token is shown once at creation.
  • Memory entries you submit — the observations, notes, and metadata you explicitly ingest via the MCP API or console.
  • Service logs — minimal operational logs (timestamps, request status) for reliability and abuse prevention.

2. How we use it

  • To operate your account: sign-up verification codes, login, and token authentication.
  • To provide the service you asked for: storing, searching, and recalling your memory entries.
  • Verification codes are delivered by email through Google's Gmail API (send-only, for the address you registered).

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties, except when required by law.
  • We do not use your content for advertising, profiling, or model training.
  • We do not read your memory entries except to run the storage/retrieval operations you request.

4. Storage, retention, deletion

Account and memory data live in our server-side database. You can revoke API tokens anytime from the console. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us (below); deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

5. Contact

Open an issue on our GitHub repository (linked from the homepage) for any privacy question or deletion request.
