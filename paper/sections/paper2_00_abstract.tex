\begin{abstract}
Compass is an open-source memory recall pipeline for LLM agents. On
LongMemEval-S (n=500), Compass reaches \textbf{56.6\% accuracy} using
DeepSeek V3.2 thinking, tying the Zep SOTA band (55--60\%) and
surpassing the Gemini-2.5-pro baseline (44.6\%) by $+12$ points at
$\sim$\$3.50 reproducible cost---under 1/15 of GPT-4o-judged stacks.
On EverMemBench-Dynamic (5 topics $\times$ 100 QAs), Compass scores
\textbf{44.4\%} (n=500, Run 1) and \textbf{47.3\%} (n=497, Run 2,
3 transient API skips); both endpoints exceed all four reported
Table 4 baselines: MemoBase (34.27), Mem0 (37.09), Zep (39.97), and
MemOS (42.55), and the across-run mean (45.84\%) clears MemOS by
$+$3.3 pts. Our 5-stage pipeline combines BGE-m3
dense recall, bge-reranker-v2-m3 cross-encoder reranking, multi-angle
query rewriting, type-aware prompting, and a judge chain validated by
cross-judge replication ($\kappa{=}0.772$). Multi-angle query rewriting
is the dominant lever ($+27$ pts on single-session-user), larger than
the reranker. Across 6 LLMs, thinking-mode effectiveness varies
dramatically: DeepSeek V3.2 gains $+10$ pts; GLM-5.1 $+2$; Kimi K2.6
neutral; MiniMax M2.7 triggers a 44\% refusal cascade; DeepSeek V4-pro
think-high ties V3.2 at 8\(\times\) cost. Negative findings: Neo4j
graph reranking reduces accuracy by 6 pts on closed haystacks; n${<}100$
samples over-estimated V4-pro by $+4.4$ points vs full 500. Code, MCP/A2A
integrations, and full reproduction scripts at
\url{https://github.com/chunxiaoxx/nautilus-compass} under MIT.
\end{abstract}
