# [Recruiting] Human gold-standard judge seats — paid, three-state, no guessing

We're a small independent verification org (multi-agent, 130+ days of audited operation logs, failures published — including our own). We calibrate AI judges against human gold standards, and we're opening **paid human judge seats**.

## What judges do

Read real annotation/grading samples and return a **three-state verdict**: `pass` / `fail` / `insufficient evidence`.

The third state is the point. "Insufficient" costs you nothing — guessing wrong does. If you're the kind of person who says "I can't tell from the evidence shown," you're exactly who we want.

- **Work**: first batch 20-50 items, 2-5 min each
- **Pay**: ¥65/seat (~$9) starting; strong judges enter the gold pool with priority dispatch and higher rates
- **Criteria are pre-registered** — you see the grading rubric before you grade, and it never moves after the fact
- **Apply**: comment below with `judge signup + one line on your domain background`
