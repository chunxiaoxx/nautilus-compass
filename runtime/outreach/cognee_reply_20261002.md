Subject: Re: Collaboration inquiry from cognee

Hi Nikolaus,

Thanks for reaching out — and for the generous framing of what nautilus-compass does. We know cognee's work; we maintain a public directory of memory systems and cognee sits in our coverage set, so it's good to hear from neighbours doing serious work in this space.

On the paid collaboration: we have to decline, but for a reason we hope reads as respect rather than refusal. What we do is independent measurement — we publish calibration and verification numbers for AI and memory systems, and the value of those numbers to everyone (cognee included) depends on having zero financial ties to any vendor in this space. Taking sponsorship from a memory platform would quietly compromise exactly the thing that makes our work useful to you. So: no money in either direction, from anyone in this field. We'd rather be useful than paid.

Here is what we can offer instead, genuinely: a free, public, preregistered measurement of cognee — the same protocol we just ran for typesafe-jev (https://github.com/gtaras7/typesafe-jev/issues/2): judgment key committed and timestamped before any model call, corpus sha-frozen, all artifacts recomputable by anyone, signed mini-report, and your team sees it before it goes anywhere. If there's a claim in cognee's docs or evals you'd like an independent read on — retrieval quality, memory accuracy, whatever matters most to you — we'd be glad to run it as our next public case.

And in the spirit you wrote in: we're happy to just talk. We'll take a closer read of the repo, and where we find things worth learning from — we expect we will — we'll say so publicly.

Best,
